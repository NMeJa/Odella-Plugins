Odella website — what to check (speed, cache, cart quantity, and registration)

Log in to wp-admin as an administrator for the first three checks. For the store and registration, also try a phone and a computer. Tick a box only if what you see matches the “You should see” line. If it does not, write down the page and what you saw instead.

Use the Georgian site. English pages are drafts and are not part of this check. Do not pay. Payment is turned off on purpose. The store is /ka/store/. The address /ka/shop/ is not the store.

1. Odella → Settings

In wp-admin, open Odella → Settings. Only an administrator sees this screen. Shop managers see the other three Odella screens and do not see Settings.

You should see: both key tests succeed, one to three languages, a prompt you can edit only while Custom is on, and Settings saved.

A problem: a test says the key is missing or the request failed, Custom off still lets you type, or Save does not confirm.

You do not type the language-tab format. The site adds that automatically when the report is built, so the language tabs keep working.

2. Is Slow?

In the top admin bar, click Is Slow?. It opens Odella → Speed Check and starts on its own. Wait. PageSpeed and the written report take a few minutes. You do not need to press Run check unless nothing starts.

You should see: 9 checks, mobile and desktop PageSpeed cards when the key worked, language tabs when Gemini ran, and a banner that warms a cold cache on its own.

A problem: the table stays empty, PageSpeed or Gemini fails after a successful key test, a cold cache sits there with no banner, or stylesheets are 404 and nobody has purged the CDN.

3. Reindex and the cache

Click Reindex in the top bar, or open Odella → Catalog & Cache (the page title is Catalog index) and press Rebuild index & warm cache. This walks the store pages. Let it finish. Do not click it again while it is busy.

You should see: indexing, then Warming X / Y, then a warmed count.

A problem: the label sticks on Reindex, never says Warming, or ends with an error and no warmed count.

4. Cart quantity on the store

Open the store. Do not open /ka/shop/. Add a priced product, for example Cardigan Dream Bordeaux (430 ₾). The cart drawer should open.

You should see: the quantity move immediately, buttons that keep working, and the Georgian drawer, logged in and logged out.

A problem: the number waits, the buttons grey out, the drawer switches to English, or /ka/shop/ was the page you tested.

5. Registration

Open ჩემი პროფილი while logged out. Use a real email address you can open. Do not use a customer’s address. Do not pay. Payment is off.

You should see: the page move on without a long wait, and a Georgian password email you can use.

A problem: the form sits there for a long time, the email never arrives, or the email is English.

If the email does not arrive, stop and say so. Do not keep making users.

6. Pricing & Rates (optional)

Open Odella → Pricing & Rates.

You should see: a last sync, the hidden-product list (or a line that none are hidden), and a successful sync if you pressed the button.

A problem: the sync button reports an error, or a product you know has a lari price is listed as hidden.

What this check does not cover

The shop filter, checkout phone, and account tabs are in the previous checklist. English drafts stay unpublished. Payment and shipping stay off, so do not try to pay.
