Odella website — what to check (shop, cart, checkout, and account)

Open each link on a phone and on a computer. On a wide screen, also try a window about 2560 pixels wide if you can. Tick the box only if what you see matches the “You should see” line. If it does not, write down the page and what you saw instead.

Use the Georgian site. English pages are drafts and are not part of this check. Do not pay. Payment is turned off on purpose. The cart page now opens at /ka/cart/.

1. Store filter, search, and sort

Open the store.

You should see: category links, Georgian search and sort, and a price on every card you can add.

A problem: the old WOOF filter is back, a card has no price, or sort does not change the order.

A category you open by its address still loads on the server. Filter clicks use a saved product list. If a product you just edited is missing from a filter click, use the admin-bar Reindex button or Products → Catalog index. The direct category address still shows the live list.

2. A product with no price cannot be bought

Open the unpriced Morbido long kimono.

You should see: the description, and nothing you can press to buy it.

A problem: an add button or a price appears on Morbido.

3. Adding a priced product

Open Cardigan Dream Bordeaux (430 ₾).

You should see: the cardigan in the drawer, and a size chosen before Pera can be added.

A problem: the cardigan button does nothing, or Pera adds with no size.

4. The cart drawer

With at least one product in the cart, open the store again (a normal visit, not a private window is fine). Then reload the page.

You should see: Georgian drawer text, a count that survives reload, and names that are not sliced in half.

A problem: the count stays on an old number after reload, remove does nothing, or the drawer is in English.

Please read the Georgian lines above and say if any wording should change. Those are the texts now on the site.

5. Checkout phone

Put one priced product in the cart. Open checkout. Fill the other required fields with test details. Do not place an order.

You should see: +995 on one line, a clear error for a number that does not start with 5, and no way to pay.

A problem: the flag sits above the number, the field is pink, a bad number is accepted, or a real order is created.

6. The cart page

Add Cardigan Dream Bordeaux. In the notice, press კალათის ნახვა.

You should see: a Georgian cart page that stays in step with the drawer.

A problem: the address is “page not found”, the name is cut off, the total stays on the old number, or checkout has a different product.

7. My Account and registration

Open ჩემი პროფილი while logged out. Use an email address you can read. Do not use a customer’s address.

You should see: Georgian login and registration, an email you can use, and five account tabs.

A problem: no registration form, the email never arrives, the email is English, or Downloads or Payment methods is in the menu.

If the registration email does not arrive, stop and say so. Do not keep making users.

8. Page headings

You should see: the page’s own Georgian heading, and nothing English from the theme above it.

A problem: an English title is back, or the Georgian heading sits under the header.

These three have no heading now, on purpose, until you say what they should say: Shipping, the blog archive, and any missing page (the 404). Write the Georgian title you want, or say they should stay blank.

What this check does not cover

Prices abroad (euros and dollars) are in the prices checklist, not this one. English drafts stay unpublished. Payment and shipping stay off, so checkout cannot finish an order.
