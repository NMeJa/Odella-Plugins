Odella website — what to check (speed and scrolling)

Open each link on a phone and on a computer. Tick the box only if what you see matches the “You should see” line. If it does not, write down the page and what you saw instead.

Use the Georgian site: https://odella.ge/ka/. English pages are not part of this check.

1. Homepage scrolling

Open the homepage.

You should see: four screens that snap, then a footer you can read to the end.

A problem: the footer is cut off, a fifth dot is still there, or one swipe skips a screen.

2. Product photos

Open Cardigan Dream Bordeaux and the store.

You should see: a clear garment photo, and the large picture when you tap it.

A problem: the main photo is obviously blurry, or the tap does not open the large picture.

3. Moving from page to page

You should see: a short white flash with the logo, then the next page.

A problem: the white screen stays, the logo never appears, or Back leaves a white page.

What this check does not cover

PageSpeed scores and the long cache warm are a separate run. The homepage still has no PageSpeed score because the first screen is a video. That was already true before these changes. Do not mark the homepage as failed for that.
