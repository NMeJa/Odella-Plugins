Odella website — what to check (prices)

Do these from Georgia first, with no VPN. Then repeat the marked steps with a VPN. Tick a box only if the page matches the “You should see” line. If it does not, write the page address and the price you saw.

Prices in Georgia use the lari sign (₾). Abroad, Germany should show euros (€) and the United States should show dollars ($). A converted foreign price ends in 9 (149, 159, 169). A price someone typed by hand does not get changed.

1. Products with no price stay out of the shop

These products are still on the site. They must not appear as cards you can buy.

You should see: priced cards in the store, an empty long-kimono list, and the direct Morbido page with no price.

A problem: a card in the store, search, or related products has a blank price.

2. Georgia prices did not change

No VPN. You are in Georgia.

A problem: a Georgian visitor sees € or $, or the cardigan is no longer 430 ₾.

3. Abroad, the price is converted and ends in 9

Use a VPN. Open the same cardigan: Cardigan Dream Bordeaux.

A 90 ₾ scarf is about 39 € in Germany and 39 $ in the United States. Both end in 9. That is expected.

A problem: Germany or the United States still shows 430, or the cart total is different from the price on the product page.

4. A price typed by hand wins

This step is for the person who edits products in WP Sheet Editor.

  1. WP Sheet Editor → Products → Columns visibility / Custom fields. Turn on _eurozone_regular_price and _global_regular_price. Rename them EUR price and USD price. If the columns are missing, type one manual price on the normal product screen first, then turn the columns on.
  2. On one test product, set EUR price to 200 and save.
  3. With the VPN in Germany, that product shows 200 € exactly. It does not become 209.
  4. Clear that EUR price and save. Germany shows the converted price ending in 9 again.

5. A new product with no price does not stay in the shop

In WordPress → Products → Add New. Name it TEST unpriced. Leave the price empty. Click Publish.

Do not draft the 50 older products that already have no price. Editing only their description must leave them Published. They stay hidden from the store.
